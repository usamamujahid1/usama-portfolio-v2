SHT 02

About me

I found my lane early: construction estimation — the discipline of pricing a building before a single brick is laid.

I'm Usama Mujahid, from Darazinda, Tehsil Darazinda, Dera Ismail Khan, Pakistan, currently pursuing a BS in Civil Engineering at COMSATS University Islamabad, Abbottabad Campus. My strongest interests lie in construction estimation, quantity surveying, BOQ preparation and project management — turning drawings into measured quantities, and quantities into costs that clients and project teams can plan around.

Currently at BYH, I prepare quantity takeoffs and cost estimates for real residential projects — brickwork, concrete, tile and plaster — running digital takeoffs in PlanSwift, building reusable Excel calculators, and using ChatGPT and Claude to cross-check assumptions and document every estimate clearly.

Outside engineering, I love travelling, exploring new places, photography and creating visual content — a habit of looking closely that carries straight back into reading drawings.

CAREER OBJECTIVE

A successful Project Manager in the construction industry — built on a strong foundation of estimation, quantity surveying and BOQ expertise.
